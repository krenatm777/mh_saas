Payment flow

How payment and the client journey work

The full client path: from submitting a request to accessing the platform. Card payments are processed by the PayLink internet-acquiring system; settlement is in tenge (KZT).

  1. 1. Request

    The client fills in the request form on the site (order.html). The data is sent to MusharaqaHub; the client sees a confirmation page.

  2. 2. Offer and invoice

    A specialist clarifies the scope, the parties agree the cost, and the client receives an offer and an invoice with the amount in tenge (KZT).

  3. 3. Checkout

    In the personal account the client opens the payment page (payment.html): order number, service, amount in KZT, and a "Pay by card" button.

  4. 4. Secure payment (PayLink)

    The client is redirected to the secure PayLink page, enters card details (Visa / Mastercard) and passes 3-D Secure. Card data is not transferred to or stored by MusharaqaHub.

  5. 5. Result

    PayLink processes the payment and redirects the client back: to the success page (success.html) or the failure page (fail.html). An electronic payment confirmation is sent to the client.

  6. 6. Access to the platform

    After successful payment, access to the personal account and the ordered modules is granted; processing of the IP object continues through its stages.

  7. 7. Refund

    A refund, where applicable, is made to the same bank card under the Refund Terms, in tenge (KZT).
