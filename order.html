3. Cost and terms

Base access cost — 15 000 USD / ≈ 6 750 000 ₸. Card payments are processed in tenge (KZT) at the NBK rate on the payment date. Individual services and modules are also available — from 500 000 ₸. The final cost, scope and licence term are agreed individually based on the analysis of your IP object and fixed in the contract (offer). Payment can be made in tranches.

4. Consents and confirmations

By clicking "Submit request" you confirm your agreement with the personal-data processing terms and the terms of access to the MusharaqaHub platform.

What happens after you submit

  • Your request is registered and receives a unique number.
  • Within 1 (one) business day a MusharaqaHub specialist contacts you to clarify the parameters of your IP object.
  • Based on the analysis, an individual offer and a contract (offer) are prepared.
  • After the terms are agreed, payment is made by bank card via a secure payment page and access to the platform is granted.
